# Modules

## Composition

The Standard Registry responsibility is decomposed where durable child boundaries have exclusive primary ownership and independent contracts; immediate children remain contained by this parent while cross-Module dependencies stay authoritative in contract.json.

## Modules

### [Standard Registry Testing](../mods/testing/README.md)

## Coordination

The immediate children collectively fulfill the Standard Registry responsibility through the parent boundary. Their conceptual contributions compose here without restating or replacing the typed relationship graph declared by their Module contracts.
