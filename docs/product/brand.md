# 23 — Brand and Public Identity

**Status:** Normative brand and public identity
**Authority class:** Brand

## Organization

**GitHub organization:** `fortress-engineering`
**Display name:** Fortress Engineering

**Organization description:**

> Open-source home of the Fortress Engineering Standard and its tooling for coherent, auditable, certifiable software development.

## Repositories

### `fortress-engineering/fortress`

**Description:**

> Engineering control plane for auditing, governing, managing, and certifying coherent software systems under the Fortress Engineering Standard.

The flagship repository owns the implementation, normative standard source, machine-readable schemas, conformance suites, developer architecture, and core governance.

### `fortress-engineering/website`

**Description:**

> Canonical public website, documentation, standard reference, rule catalog, and learning hub for Fortress.

The website is the public/user surface for learning, adoption, rules, profiles, tutorials, upgrade guides, and standard reference.

### `fortress-engineering/.github`

**Description:**

> Organization profile, shared community resources, governance, and cross-repository standards for the Fortress ecosystem.

## Product naming

**Formal standard:** Fortress Engineering Standard
**Product/short name:** Fortress
**CLI:** `fortress`

Fortress should not be branded primarily as a security product. The descriptor “Engineering Standard” and “engineering control plane” should consistently frame the name.

## Core product statement

Recommended long-form positioning:

> **Fortress is an executable engineering control plane for software repositories. It models the codebase as a governed system, unifies development and operational tooling, enforces architectural and quality standards, manages resumable project workflows, and produces content-addressed certification from development through release.**

Shorter category statement:

> **Multilingual codebase auditing and certification for hyper-strict, coherent software development.**

Philosophical statement:

> **Fortress turns engineering architecture from an informal design intention into a certifiable property of the repository.**

## Canonical logo — Version F

Version F is the approved canonical geometry direction.

The Fortress Engineering logo is a **minimal abstract architectural lattice** representing engineered coherence, explicit structure, interdependent components, and a software system whose relationships are governed rather than accidental.

The mark must communicate Fortress as an engineering standard and control plane, not as a cybersecurity product. It is not a literal fortress, wall, castle, shield, lock, or protective enclosure. It resembles a deliberately constructed framework whose members occupy exact positions and visibly participate in one coherent structure.

### Geometry

The mark consists of approximately five or six major solid rectangular structural members on a strict orthogonal modular grid.

It has:

- a dominant vertical structural spine;
- a strong upper horizontal member;
- a central horizontal relationship;
- a lower horizontal structural member;
- smaller supporting vertical members that visually belong to the framework.

Every major member must feel structurally meaningful. Nothing should read as arbitrary decoration.

The mark is compact and approximately square but has **no complete enclosing perimeter**. Foundry Gold is the structure itself, not a large gold field surrounding white negative-space corridors.

The background remains visible around and between members.

### Negative space

Negative space is deliberate and open.

Avoid narrow corridors, repeated turns, or closed channels that make the icon resemble:

- a maze;
- floor plan;
- castle enclosure;
- circuit diagram;
- security boundary.

The structure should feel extensible rather than sealed.

### Subtle F

A faint abstract `F` may emerge from the dominant vertical member and horizontal beams, but it is secondary.

The perceptual order is:

1. structural framework;
2. interdependent engineered system;
3. subtle F association.

It must not become a conventional F lettermark.

### Prohibited imagery

Do not use:

- castles;
- shields;
- locks/keyholes;
- checkmarks;
- code brackets;
- terminal prompts;
- circuit traces;
- gears;
- literal dependency graph nodes;
- decorative technology clichés.

### Rendering

Canonical logo rendering:

- solid flat Foundry Gold `#C88A18`;
- Blueprint Paper `#F5F3ED` background;
- no gradient;
- no shadow;
- no glow;
- no bevel;
- no texture;
- no metallic reflection;
- crisp vector-like square corners and alignment.

The silhouette must remain identifiable at 16–32 px favicon/GitHub-avatar scale and in monochrome.

## Visual meaning

The logo should communicate:

> **Every component has a place, every relationship is deliberate, and the whole remains coherent because its structure is explicit.**

This is the visual expression of Fortress's central idea: **coherence by design**.
